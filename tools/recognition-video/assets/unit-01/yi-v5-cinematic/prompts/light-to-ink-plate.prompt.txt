Primary request: A single hero composition: traditional Chinese xuan paper substrate with a 米字格 grid watermark, AND on top of the grid, a single horizontal stroke that is mid-transition from warm-gold light to warm-deep ink. Half of the stroke (the left half) still glows like warm light; the right half has settled as warm-deep ink. This is the "light becoming ink" moment — the visual translation of metaphor into character.

Reference image usage: This plate uses three reference images: (1) the v5 style-anchor mood board for palette/brush/paper lockdown, (2) the v5 xuan-paper substrate reference for the EXACT same paper texture (fiber lines, screen marks, corner aging, tea-stain ring placement) — the paper here MUST match the paper of `ink-glyph-plate` and `hand-tracing-plate` so consecutive shots feel like the same physical sheet, and (3) the v5 mizige-watermark grid reference for the EXACT same grid shape, position, line weight, and tan watermark color #c9b48a.

Subject (strict layered contract): A single composition painted as 3 stacked layers from back to front:
  Layer 1 — Xuan paper substrate (full bleed): The same warm-cream xuan paper #f5ead0 from the project's substrate reference. Subtle vertical handmade-paper fiber lines, very faint horizontal screen lines, soft warm-gray corner aging fading inward (corners ~12% darker), one very subtle tea-stain ring near the upper-right (about 80px wide, opacity ~8%, warm-brown #c9b48a). Visible painterly oil-on-paper grain throughout. The center 60% of the frame should remain visually quiet so the grid + stroke can dominate.
  Layer 2 — 米字格 (mizige) grid watermark: The same grid from the project's mizige-watermark reference. One single mizige, perfectly square, centered horizontally, vertically centered at 55% of frame height, outer dimension ~62% of frame width. Composed of 4 outer edges + 1 horizontal mid + 1 vertical mid + 2 diagonals (8 line segments forming the 米 pattern inside a square). All grid lines warm-tan watermark #c9b48a, ~2px thick, painterly soft watermark print, NOT bold.
  Layer 3 — The transitioning stroke (the hero element): A single horizontal stroke laid across the horizontal mid-line of the mizige grid, occupying about 80% of the grid's interior width (so the stroke starts ~10% inside the left edge of the grid and ends ~10% inside the right edge of the grid). The stroke has:
    - LEFT HALF (left 50% of stroke length): still glowing like warm-gold light. Brightest core #fff3c8 along centerline, fading outward through #ffd071, halo #f4b06a, soft outer edge fading into transparent. NO hard edge — this half reads as light. Subtle upward-drifting soft mist wisps just above and below the left-half stroke, in cream-warm #ebdfc4 at ~10% opacity, suggesting the light is still alive and moving.
    - RIGHT HALF (right 50% of stroke length): settled as warm-deep ink #1f1a14. Hard but painterly edge (not vector razor-sharp; ink-on-paper soft feathering into paper fibers at the edges). Subtle thickening at the start of the right half where it transitions from light to ink (a soft "起笔" thickening), even body, subtle pressure release toward the right end ("收笔" taper). The ink shows the finest hint of feathering into paper fibers but the stroke body is opaque and confident.
    - TRANSITION ZONE (middle ~12% of stroke length): a soft gradient zone where light becomes ink. The colors shift from warm-gold halo #f4b06a fading through a warm-amber midpoint #b88848 fading into warm-deep ink #1f1a14. NO hard discontinuity — the transition reads as light cooling and crystallizing into ink. This zone should have a slight downward "drip" or "settle" feeling — maybe a tiny suggestion of ink pooling slightly below the centerline at the transition midpoint, like the moment ink first touches paper.

Style/medium: Oil-on-paper painterly hand. The paper and grid are calm and matte; the left-half light is luminous and atmospheric; the right-half ink is matte and tactile. The transition is the hero.
